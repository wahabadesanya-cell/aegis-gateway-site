Frequently asked questions

What is an MCP connection?

An MCP (Model Context Protocol) connection is a single integration between an LLM client — such as Claude Desktop or a custom AI assistant — and an internal system (API, database, service). Each connection is protected by Aegis Guard in real time.

When will Aegis Guard be available?

Aegis Guard is currently in private early access. Joining the waitlist locks in early access pricing and gives you priority onboarding when we open the next cohort.

Can I switch tiers after onboarding?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time — the change takes effect immediately and you are billed the pro-rata difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

Do you offer a trial?

Early access customers receive a 30-day evaluation period. Enterprise evaluations are scoped individually — contact us to arrange a proof-of-concept in your environment.

What does "compliance implementation partner" mean?

Enterprise customers get direct access to Adesanya AI Advisory for EU AI Act and ISO 42001 guidance alongside the technical deployment — legal and governance counsel built into the engagement.

Is on-premise deployment available on lower tiers?

On-premise and private cloud deployment is available on the Enterprise tier. Starter and Professional run on Aegis Guard's managed cloud infrastructure with data residency in the EU.

Not sure which tier fits?

Tell us how many AI systems you're running and what compliance obligations are on your desk. We'll recommend the right tier — no sales pressure.
